## High Meat
Cures depression and constipation
### Ingredients
- raw meat (muscle meat, liver, kidney, brains, ...)

Put in a mason jar, seal tightly, put it in your fridge.
Take it outside and wave it around every 3-4 days to exchange the air molecules.
DO NOT OPEN inide, it will stink up your house.
Eat at your leisure, or ferment it for months/years.
For more information, read [Aajonus Vonderplanitz](https://www.amazon.com/We-Want-to-Live/dp/B001UIXBG6).
